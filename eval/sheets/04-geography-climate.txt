IGCSE GEOGRAPHY - Topic 2.4: Hot Desert Climates

Hot desert environments, such as the Rub' al Khali in the Arabian Peninsula, receive less than 250 mm of precipitation annually. Diurnal temperature ranges are extreme: daytime maxima frequently exceed 45 degrees Celsius, whereas nocturnal temperatures may fall below 10 degrees Celsius owing to the absence of cloud cover, which permits rapid radiative heat loss. Xerophytic vegetation exhibits adaptations including extensive root systems, waxy cuticles and reduced leaf surface area to minimise transpiration.
