Homework Sheet 3

Remember: to convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply the numerator by 100 and divide by the denominator. Equivalent fractions represent the same proportion of a whole.

1. Express 3/4 as a decimal.
2. Calculate 25% of 360.
3. A bakery sold 48 loaves on Monday and 1/3 of those loaves were wholemeal. How many wholemeal loaves were sold?
4. Order the following values from smallest to largest: 0.6, 2/5, 55%, 0.45
5. Amira scored 18 out of 24 on a quiz. What percentage did she score?
6. Which fraction is equivalent to 6/8?
   (a) 2/3   (b) 3/4   (c) 4/6   (d) 5/8
