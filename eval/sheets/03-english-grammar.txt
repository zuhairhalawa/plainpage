ENGLISH LANGUAGE - Grade 5
Worksheet: Subordinate Clauses and Conjunctions

A subordinate clause contains a verb but cannot stand alone as a complete sentence. It is introduced by a subordinating conjunction such as because, although, when, if or unless. For example: "Although it was raining, the children played outside."

Part A: Underline the subordinate clause in each sentence.
1. We stayed indoors because the wind was strong.
2. When the bell rings, line up quietly.
3. The cake will burn unless you take it out of the oven.

Part B: Join each pair of sentences using the conjunction given in brackets.
4. I was tired. I finished my homework. (although)
5. You water the plant. It will grow. (if)

Part C: Write your own sentence that contains a subordinate clause beginning with "when".
