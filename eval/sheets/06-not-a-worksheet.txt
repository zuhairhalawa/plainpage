SPICY LENTIL SOUP (serves 4)

Ingredients: 200 g red lentils, 1 onion, 2 cloves of garlic, 1 teaspoon cumin, 800 ml vegetable stock, juice of half a lemon.

Method: Fry the chopped onion and garlic in a little oil for 5 minutes. Add the cumin and cook for 1 minute. Add the lentils and stock, bring to the boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until soft. Blend until smooth and finish with the lemon juice. Season to taste.
