BIOLOGY - The Circulatory System (Grade 8)

The human heart is a muscular organ comprising four chambers: two atria and two ventricles. Deoxygenated blood returns from the body via the vena cava into the right atrium, and is then pumped by the right ventricle to the lungs through the pulmonary artery. Oxygenated blood returns to the left atrium via the pulmonary vein. The left ventricle has a thicker muscular wall because it must generate sufficient pressure to pump blood around the entire body. A resting adult heart typically beats between 60 and 100 times per minute.
