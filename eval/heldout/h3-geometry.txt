Maths Year 7 - Area and Perimeter        Date: __________

The perimeter of a shape is the total distance around its edges. The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width, and is measured in square units such as cm2 or m2. The area of a triangle is half of the base multiplied by the perpendicular height.

Q1. A rectangle is 12 cm long and 5 cm wide. Calculate its perimeter.
Q2. Calculate the area of the same rectangle.
Q3. A triangle has a base of 10 m and a perpendicular height of 6 m. Find its area.
Q4. A square garden has an area of 49 m2. What is the length of one side?
Q5. Which of these is the correct unit for area?
    a) cm    b) cm2    c) cm3
