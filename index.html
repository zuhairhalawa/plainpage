Plain Page

Worksheets rebuilt for every reader. Checked, not guessed.

A worksheet a screen reader can't read is a lesson someone misses.

Take a photo of a worksheet. Plain Page rebuilds it as a real page: headings a screen reader can jump between, answer boxes with labels, large clear type, plain English, and read-aloud. Then it checks that every number and key term made it across.

  • Read on your device. Photos are read in your browser and never uploaded. Names and contact details are blacked out before any text is sent.
  • Nothing lost. Every number and key term is checked against the worksheet. If the plain version drops one, you see the original wording instead.
  • Yours to keep. Save the rebuilt page as one file that works offline, on any device, with any screen reader.
